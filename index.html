16 GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

Still curious? We’re happy to talk.

What is RadeNext HRMS?+

RadeNext is a connected people operations platform that brings employee information, attendance, time off, payroll workflows, hiring, and workforce insights into one workspace.

Who is RadeNext designed for?+

RadeNext is designed for growing teams that want their people processes to feel connected and easier to navigate. Talk with our team about your size, locations, and needs.

Can our employees use RadeNext themselves?+

Yes. The employee self-service experience gives people a place to review their profile, manage time off, check payslips, and stay connected to their work life.

Can RadeNext fit our existing workflows?+

RadeNext is built to support adaptable people workflows. Book a demo and we can talk through your team's current processes and requirements.

How does RadeNext handle payroll?+

Payroll workflows bring employee changes and review steps together. Availability and local payroll support can vary, so our team can help confirm what's right for your region.

How can I learn more about security?+

Security and privacy details depend on your deployment and requirements. Contact our team to discuss controls, data handling, and any review your organization needs.
